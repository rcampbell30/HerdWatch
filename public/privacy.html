Privacy policy

How Immunity Map handles visitor information.

Last updated: 20 July 2026.

Immunity Map is a public-information website and does not require visitor accounts. The vaccination dataset is aggregate public-health data; it contains no household-level or individual vaccination records.

Technical data

The hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested pages and timestamps for security, reliability and traffic measurement.

Analytics and advertising

If analytics or advertising services are enabled, they may process device information or use cookies according to their own policies. Any consent control required for non-essential cookies should be shown before those technologies are activated.

External links

The site links to NHS, UKHSA, journals and other third-party services. Their privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Questions

Contact the maintainer through the Immunity Map GitHub repository.
